Common questions

Do I need my own API keys?
No. AI generation is fully handled by Clayforge — no API keys needed from you.
Which clay bodies are supported?
All major clay bodies — Earthenware, Stoneware, Porcelain, Bone China, Terracotta, Raku, Ball Clay, Fireclay, Paperclay, and more. Available on all plans.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your account page at any time. No hidden fees.
How do I pay?
Payments are processed via Paystack — card, bank transfer, or USSD. International cards are accepted; your bank handles currency conversion automatically.
Who owns the designs?
You do. All designs belong entirely to you.
