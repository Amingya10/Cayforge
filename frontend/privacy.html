Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clayforge ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our platform. By using Clayforge, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: your name, email address, password (stored as a salted hash), and preferred currency.
  • Design data: the text prompts you enter, the AI-generated specifications, and the visual renders associated with your account.
  • Payment information: handled entirely by Paystack. We never see or store your card details. We retain transaction references and the plan you subscribed to.
  • Usage information: generation counts, the date you signed up, the date of your last activity, and basic technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and debugging purposes.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the Service (accounts, design library, billing);
  • Send AI prompts to Anthropic and OpenAI to generate the designs you request;
  • Communicate with you about your account, payments, and material updates to the Service;
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. AI providers and your prompts

When you generate a design, the prompt you submit is sent to Anthropic (for the text specification) and OpenAI (for the image render). These providers process your prompt to produce the output and are bound by their own privacy and data handling policies. Per current Anthropic and OpenAI policies for API customers, your prompts are not used to train their models. We do not control changes to their policies, so we recommend reviewing them periodically:

  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

4. Image storage

Visual renders generated for your designs are stored on Cloudinary, a cloud media platform. Cloudinary acts as our processor and stores images on our behalf. You can delete any saved design from your Library at any time, which removes the associated image from our records.

5. Data retention

We retain your account information and saved designs for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal information and saved designs within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain certain records (such as transaction records for tax purposes).

6. Sharing your information

We share your information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate Clayforge (Railway for hosting, Vercel for the frontend, Cloudinary for image storage, Paystack for payments, Resend for transactional email, Anthropic and OpenAI for AI generation);
  • Authorities when legally required (e.g. court order or regulatory request);
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or part of our business, with notice to you.

7. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, secure environment variables for API keys, and access controls. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a data breach affecting you, we will notify you promptly.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete your account and associated personal data;
  • Export your saved designs in a portable format;
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@clayforge.app.

9. Children's privacy

Clayforge is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International users

Clayforge is operated from Nigeria. If you use the Service from outside Nigeria, your information will be transferred to and processed in Nigeria and in any country where our service providers operate. By using the Service, you consent to such transfer.

11. Cookies

Clayforge uses minimal browser storage (localStorage) to keep you signed in across sessions and to remember preferences. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising trackers.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or a notice on the Service. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at hello@clayforge.app.
